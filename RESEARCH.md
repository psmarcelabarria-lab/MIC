# MIC — Research background and current status / Investigación y estado actual

**Project:** Modelo de Integración Consciente (MIC)  
**Author:** Marcela Barría Cárdenas, clinical psychologist (Chile)  
**Status:** Conceptual framework and exploratory technical work; operationalization and empirical evaluation pending.  
**Last editorial update:** 2026-11-07

## 1. Research purpose / Propósito
MIC explores how AI can be integrated into psychology and mental-health contexts while preserving human professional judgment, confidentiality and the depth of clinical understanding. Its public-facing domains are **Soberanía**, **Secreto** and **Profundidad**. These domains are proposed conceptual organizing principles, not empirically established psychometric factors.

The project distinguishes: (a) conceptual research; (b) educational and exploratory prototypes; (c) future evaluation instruments; and (d) potential reusable software components. None of these categories should be conflated.

## 2. Work already undertaken / Trabajo realizado
- **Conceptual model:** a preprint authored by Barría Cárdenas was published on Zenodo on 12 April 2026 (version 1.0): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19528298
- **Exploratory platform questionnaire:** an external browser-based self-report prototype, *Diagnóstico MIC para Plataformas*, is available at https://iaysaludmental.com/diagnostico_plataformas_mic.html . It is a proof of concept, not a validated audit or certification.
- **Public repository:** documentation, ethics and roadmap currently exist in this GitHub repository.

## 3. The three domains / Los tres pilares
**Soberanía:** examine where meaningful decisions and oversight remain with people, including professionals and users; clarify when AI suggestions might displace judgment.

**Secreto:** examine confidentiality, sensitive information, consent, data flows, retention and disclosure, with attention to the special risks of clinical contexts.

**Profundidad:** examine whether AI-mediated interactions flatten or distort contextual, relational and clinical understanding, and how uncertainty and complexity are preserved.

These descriptions are public-facing summaries. They are not a complete publication of the theoretical model or its protected instrument specifications.

## 4. Research questions / Preguntas de investigación
1. Can the three domains be operationalized as observable, distinguishable criteria?
2. Which criteria are best assessed through documents, user testing, expert review or platform observation rather than self-report?
3. Which safety-critical conditions should be treated as non-compensatory rather than averaged into a total score?
4. What forms of reliability, validity and usability evidence are needed for each intended use?
5. Which technical components can be released openly without exposing confidential clinical data or protected assessment materials?

## 6. Responsible research boundaries
No patient data, identifiable clinical narratives, unpublished protected instrument items, scoring keys, API credentials or private datasets should be committed. Any human-participant research must have an appropriate protocol, consent and data-management arrangements before recruitment.

## 7. Next research stages
Construct mapping → expert content review → cognitive interviewing/usability → revision of item wording and scoring → reproducibility and reliability studies where appropriate → validation matched to the intended use → carefully scoped software release.
