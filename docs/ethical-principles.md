# Ethical principles / Principios éticos

1. **Human oversight / Supervisión humana:** software should not substitute clinical judgment.
2. **Privacy / Privacidad:** no real patient data in the public repository or early prototypes.
3. **Transparency / Transparencia:** document intended use, limitations, and uncertainty.
4. **Non-maleficence / No maleficencia:** avoid presenting experimental prototypes as clinically validated tools.
5. **Accessibility / Accesibilidad:** design for diverse users and learning needs.
6. **Accountability / Responsabilidad:** human professionals remain responsible for their decisions.
7. **Evidence / Evidencia:** distinguish conceptual proposals, usability findings, and empirical validation.

These are preliminary design commitments, not a completed ethical audit or regulatory compliance certification.

Estos principios son compromisos preliminares de diseño; no equivalen a una auditoría ética finalizada ni a una certificación de cumplimiento normativo.
