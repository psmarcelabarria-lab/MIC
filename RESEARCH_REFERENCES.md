## Evidence extraction template
For every future source, record:
1. Full citation, DOI or stable URL and access date.
2. Type of source (empirical study, systematic review, normative guidance, commentary).
3. Population, setting, technology and jurisdiction.
4. Main findings, strength of evidence and limitations.
5. MIC domain(s) and operational criterion(s) informed.
6. Whether the source supports a normative recommendation, a design hypothesis or an empirical claim.
7. Reviewer and review date.

## Important distinction
Normative standards, product guidelines, research evidence and prototype design decisions are different forms of support. They must not be presented interchangeably. Do not assert regulatory compliance or psychometric validity merely because a feature maps to a guideline.
